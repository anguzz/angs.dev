Some bands I’ve seen and thoughts on them

  • 311

    Their vocals aged very well and sound very refined, great energy, saw them a second time for KROQ christmas at kia forum and they sounded amazing once again

  • Atmosphere

    Cool to see them in person since I heard them a lot growing up while playing some Tony Hawk Underground 2 on PlayStation 2

  • Beach Fossils

    They were awesome, saw them live in my hometown of Santa Ana with two friends and we had a great time. They were like 5 feet away since we got there early and had front row pit, and they sound just like their albums or better. Played a lot of their OG stuff and top songs.

  • Beach Goons

    Sounded great, one of my favorite performances I think. As soon as they started with the first guitar riff, the crowd went absolutely bonkers, same exact thing happend 2nd time i saw them, lost my glasses in the crowd, 10/10 would do again

  • Black Flag

    Saw them in my hometown of Santa ana at the observatory, the pit was super crowded the whole time and they sounded amazing live. Second time I saw them again in Santa ana and they sounded great.

  • Buck O' Nine

    Really liked the venue (Alex's Bar in Long Beach) they have great vibes and played lots of their top hits which was awesome

  • Cigarettes after sex

    saw them at kia forum, completely sold out so it was pretty packed, sounded pretty good live though! got the tickets pre-sale about 1+ year in advanced

  • Circle Jerks

    sounded sick live

  • Dead Kennedys

    lots of energy, enjoyed seeing them live and seeing them play chemical warfare live

  • Death Lens

    The most amount of crowd diving and surfing I've seen in a while

  • Descendents

    energy was crazy, went to the show half dead with bronchitis but still had fun

  • Enjoy

    Pretty good, and the tickets were mega cheap when I saw them in 2018-ish. Also, a small venue, so they were like a couple of feet away, The Second time I saw them they were also really good live. The third time I saw them they had really good energy, the crowd was really into the performance and it was so crowded and sold out.

  • Foxtide

    Bought the tickets day of, for the heck of it since I was on the fence about seeing em. They sounded really amazing live and the jazz guitar chords were so sick in person. Pretty worth and would def see again

  • Good Morning

    Sounded just like the album recordings, and they were like 2 feet from me in a small venue. I saw them right before everything shut down for COVID

  • Hippie Sabotage

    Saw them on 4/20, which made it a very lit show

  • HOMESHAKE

    Pretty awesome, Peter's vocals sound just as good live as the recordings, and its crazy how much control he has over them. He doesn't play all his songs like the recordings so the live performance is pretty unique

  • Jpegmafia

    Super hype, the crowd was super hyped

  • Long Beach Dub Allstars

    One of my favorite performances in a while and they sounded so great live in a small venue. Very much enjoyed their energy and vibe, definitely makes me wnat to go to more reggae shows if possible going into 2025.

  • Mac DeMarco

    Was pretty sick, some random guy gave us front his front row tickets so Mac was like 20 feet away and could see him very clearly. His vocals sounded amazing live and he had such great energy, 100/10 been wanting to see my fav artist 4ever

  • No Vacation

    Vocals sounded amazing

  • Pennywise

    sounded good live but lots of weird old dudes during this crowd

  • Slightly Stoopid

    Really enjoyed seeing them in person, played a lot of their popular stuff, which I enjoyed and sounded great

  • Sublime

    Saw them perform for a Christmas KROQ performance at the kia forum with Jakob Nowell, Bradley's son who sounds just like him. It was pretty awesome and he sounds just like him, and is one of the bands I always wished I could see live so was a wish come true.

  • Subhumans

    saw them at a super small venue and the crowd was super sick and went in the pit the whole show and came out soaked because of how small and hot and crowded it was

  • Tiësto

    Got all the way to the front of the huge crowd, which was pretty sick, speakers were cool and mega loud and I liked feeling the vibrations in my whole body

  • The Adicts

    Pretty good performance, and a lot of their popular songs sounded good

  • The Bouncing souls

    Great performers, awesome crowd everyone was singing along, lots of old rowdy drunk dudes :D

  • The Frights

    Only played a handful of their more popular songs but sounded good!

  • The Garden

    Pretty intense pit and crowd, like 5 people fainted during their performance because it was so cramped and hot

  • The High Curbs

    Songs sounded great live, lots of energy

  • The Suicide Machines

    they played lots of their bangers and they sounded sick live

  • TV Girl

    Played a lot of their popular stuff, sounded pretty good, no complaints

  • TWRP

    Great performers, love how they interact with the crowd and sounded amazing, saw them again in 2024 for their digital nightmare tour and got VIP passes and was one of the best shows I've been to. Once again sounded 10/10

  • Vacations

    Vocals sounded just as good or better live and they made lots of jokes during performance that made it funny and enjoyable. Second time I saw them they were even better because the venue gave me a great view and I saw them from a balcony, the crowd and energy was awesome

  • Vundabar

    Sounded pretty awesome live, they made lots of jokes upstage and made everyone laugh

  • Yellow Days

    sounded a lot different live then the recordings, still good but seemed to almost freestyle a bit more compared to album recordings, which makes sense since with their jazz influence

  • Yot Club

    Sounded amazing live, prob one of the best performances i went to during 2024. The second time I saw them(twice in a year) they were just as good, I saw them alongside Vacations. 3rd time I saw them alongside vundabar they sounded great, especially in such a small venue like the glass house.

  • Zedd

    Saw him in Vegas at the Omnia nightclub, sounded super great live, got a great lightshow and was at the very front like 3 feet away. Even though he started like 1:40am and I was tired it felt like he was controlling my heart bpm with how good he was. The thing that sucked was the crowd was super rude and the nightclub was sold out or oversold to the point there was no space to move around which was frustrating when trying to do anything.
